*Q1 · Ch 2 · parties, roles and relationships*

*Q2 · Ch 3 · suppliers and inventory*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze
We are Conservas Brava. Lot T-08 of our tinned tuna was made on 3 Nov 2026: 500 tins, best before 3 Nov 2029. 300 are in bin B2 in our Vigo warehouse, 200 on the shelves of our Lugo shop.
a) Write the lot row and the inventory item rows.
b) Lot T-08 is recalled. Walk the tables, from the lot, to every tin and where it is.

*Q3 · Ch 3 · product associations and parts*
(a) Apply · (b) Evaluate

a) Write the rows in our product association table.
b) We keep every kind of association in this one table. Name two things that costs us.

*Q4 · Ch 4 · orders, items and order parties*
(a) Analyze · (b) Evaluate
We are Imprenta Sol, a print shop. Our rule: a sales order has exactly one bill-to. Order 640 was keyed with two bill-to parties: Café Luna and its parent, Grupo Luna.
a) Where does "exactly one bill-to" live in our model, and what does it do about order 640?
b) A colleague says: "Block the insert, so order 640 can't be saved at all." What does each way give, and what does each cost?

*Q5 · Ch 4 · order adjustments*
(a) Apply · (b) Understand
We are Joyería Plata. Order 650: a ring at €120 and two chains at €30 each, with 10% off the ring. On the whole order: 5% off for paying cash, and a €10 gift wrap.
a) Write the adjustment rows.
b) What is the 5% taken from, and why that base?

*Q6 · Ch 3 · product features*
(a) Apply · (b) Understand
We are Muebles Olmo. Our Olmo 3 sofa always has oak legs. It comes in grey, blue or green fabric, and you must pick one. Cushions come with it unless you ask to leave them out. You can add a matching footstool for €90, which we also sell on its own.
a) Write how the legs, the fabrics and the cushions apply to the sofa.
b) Is the footstool a feature of the sofa? Why?

*Q7 · Ch 4 · orders, items and order parties*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze

Line 1: 10 kg of beans to the hotel's kitchen, Calle Sur 2. Line 2: a grinder to their beach bar, Paseo 8; call Luis on 600 111 222 about that delivery.
a) Write where each address, e-mail and phone goes.
b) The day before delivery, the hotel moves line 1 to its new kitchen, Calle Sur 4. What changes, and what doesn't?
