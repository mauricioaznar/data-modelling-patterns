Q1 · Ch 4 · order terms or adjustments · (a) Apply, (b) Understand
We are Quesería Valle, a cheese maker. Our purchase order 40 to Granja Roble, our milk
supplier: line 1, 2,000 litres of milk; line 2, 50 kg of rennet. The order lists:
- if the milk arrives late, Granja Roble pays us 3% of that line's value
- the milk is delivered within 2 days
- the rennet can't be returned once opened
- €25 for refrigerated transport
a) For each one, write the row: table, on the order or which line, type, value.
b) Your rows hold a bare 3 and a bare 2. Where does a reader learn that one is a percentage
and the other is days?

Q2 · Ch 4 · a stored status and its fact · (a) Evaluate, (b) Understand
We are Panadería Trigo. Sales order 120 to Café Norte has a "shipped" status row dated
14 Nov. The shipment records show nothing for order 120: the goods are still in our
warehouse, waiting for flour.
a) Which one do you trust, and what do you do about the other?
b) After that, what is the order's current status, and how does a query find it?

Q3 · Ch 4 · a line that waits on a purchase · (a) Apply, (b) Understand
We are Ferretería Nudo, a hardware shop. Sales order 130 to Constructora Lar: line 1,
40 bags of cement; line 2, 10 drills. The cement is in stock; the drills aren't. On 5 Nov we
record that the drills are waiting, and the same day we place purchase order 55 with
Herramientas Sur: line 1, 25 drills (10 for Constructora Lar, 15 for our shelves).
a) Write the rows that record the wait and the link between the two orders: table, what
each one points at, and when.
b) Where does "10 of the 25 are for Constructora Lar" live, if anywhere?

Q4 · Ch 4 · roles on the order and the line · Apply
We are Muebles Encina, office furniture. Laura, the office manager at Gestoría Prado, phones
in sales order 140; our rep Tomás takes the call and gets the whole commission. Gestoría
Prado pays.
Line 1: 8 desks, delivered to the gestoría's new branch at Calle Sauce 4 and assembled
there by our partner Montajes Río; the branch staff will use them.
Line 2: 1 bookcase, delivered to Laura's home at Calle Pino 9, a gift from the gestoría;
she uses it.
Write the role rows: on the order or which line, the party, the type (and any share).

Q5 · Ch 4 · amounts, percentages and their base · (a) Apply, (b) Apply, (c) Understand
We are Floristería Alba. Sales order 150: line 1, 20 bouquets at €15 each; line 2, 5 vases
at €8 each. The bouquets have €2 off each (a spring promotion). The whole order gets 10% off
for a loyal customer, and delivery is €12.
a) Write each adjustment row: on the order or which line, type, amount or percentage.
b) How many euros is the 10%, and what is the order's total?
c) Why that base for the 10%?

Q6 · Ch 2 · specific or generic relationships · (a) Evaluate, (b) Apply
Academia Brillo, a language school (our system), keeps its relationships with others: the
teachers it employs, the families who are its customers, partner schools abroad for
exchanges, and the publishers it buys books from. A developer proposes one table per kind
(employment, customer_relationship, exchange_partnership, publisher_relationship), each
with two party ids and dates.
a) What does that cost the school, and what would you build instead?
b) Each exchange partnership has a yearly fee that changes every year. Where does it go?

Q7 · Ch 3 · category rollups and classification · Apply
Tienda Raíz's catalogue (our system) has the categories Food, Drinks, Organic and Organic
drinks; Organic drinks belongs both under Drinks and under Organic. A kombucha is sold as an
organic drink, and from 1 to 31 December it's also shown under Gifts.
Write the rollup rows and the kombucha's classification rows (with dates and which one is
primary).
