Q1 · Ch 4 · order terms or adjustments · Apply
We are Tostadero Brío, a coffee roaster. Sales order 90 to Restaurante Ola is already
placed: line 1, 30 kg of beans; line 2, 2 grinders. The order confirmation lists:
a) the invoice is paid within 15 days
b) 4% off the whole order, a launch promotion
c) the grinders can be cancelled without penalty up to 5 days before delivery
d) €10 for delivering to the restaurant
For each one, write the row: table, on the order or which line, type, value.

Q2 · Ch 4 · order status · (a) Apply, (b) Understand
Same roaster. Sales order 91 to Hotel Mar: line 1, 50 kg of beans; line 2, a small roasting
machine. 3 Nov: the order is approved. 4 Nov: the hotel cancels the roasting machine; the
beans stay. 6 Nov: the beans are shipped.
a) Write the status rows: table, type, what each one points at, and when.
b) On 7 Nov, what is line 1's current status, and how does a query find it?

Q3 · Ch 4 · a stored status and its fact · (a) Evaluate, (b) Understand
Mercado Lume's system has an "invoiced" status row on sales order 75, dated 10 Nov. The
invoice record itself is dated 12 Nov.
a) Which date is right, and what do you do about the other one?
b) Why can storing a status like this go wrong, and why might you store it anyway?

Q4 · Ch 4 · roles on the order and the line · Apply
We are Clima Sur, an air-conditioning company. Pablo, the purchasing manager at Hotel Faro,
places sales order 82 by e-mail. Our rep Inés handles the sale and splits the commission
50/50 with Raúl, another of our reps. Grupo Faro, the hotel's parent company, pays.
Line 1: 6 air conditioners, delivered to the hotel and installed there by our partner Frío
Norte; the hotel uses them.
Line 2: 2 packs of spare filters, delivered to Grupo Faro's maintenance depot, whose team
uses them.
Write the role rows: on the order or which line, the party, the type (and any share).

Q5 · Ch 3 · product, feature or neither · Apply; Understand for the whys
Imprenta Sol is a print shop (our catalogue). It prints and sells business cards. It offers
a logo design service. It sells reams of paper that it buys from a paper mill. Customers can
choose a gloss finish on their business cards. It prints on a press it bought last year.
Which are products (and good or service), which are features, and which are not products at
all? Say why for anything that isn't a product.

Q6 · Ch 4 · contact mechanisms on the order and the line · Apply
We are Librería Atlas, a bookshop. Colegio Pinar's secretary places sales order 84 from
secretaria@pinar.example; we take it at pedidos@atlas.example. The invoice goes to the
school at Calle Mayor 5.
Line 1, 30 maths books: delivered to the school's annex at Calle Olmo 2; for that delivery,
call the annex caretaker on 600 111 222.
Line 2, 1 teacher's guide: delivered to Calle Mayor 5.
Write the contact mechanism rows: table, on the order or which line, the mechanism, the
purpose.

Q7 · Ch 3 · base prices and feature prices · Apply; Understand for the why
Bicis Alba's catalogue (our system): the Ruta e-bike's base price is €1,200 for anyone and
€1,140 for customers in Catalonia. Its rear rack, a feature, is €60 for anyone and €45 for
Club customers (a party type). Marta, a Club member who lives in Girona (in Catalonia),
orders a Ruta with the rack.
Which price rows match, which ones win and why, and what does she pay?
