*Q5 · Ch 4 · orders, items and order parties*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze
Read first (a reinforce, so this one is warm): a count rule like "one placing customer per sales order" lives in a data-quality check, not on the rule table. Ours groups the order's role rows by order and hat and lists every pair with more than one party (4.5's "more than one of a job the columns held once"). The rule table says only which hats an order kind allows, and which standing role the wearer needs: one row per hat and standing role, dated.
We are Taller Roble. Sales order 700's line 2 was keyed with two ship-to parties: Hotel Pinar and Grupo Pinar.
a) What catches it, grouped by what, and what happens to line 2's rows?
b) The boss now wants "at most two salespeople per sales order", and a salesperson may be our EMPLOYEE or an outside AGENT. Where does the 2 go, and why not as a column on the rule table?

*Q6 · Ch 3 · products*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze

a) Which are products (a good or a service), and which aren't products, and what are they instead?
b) A colleague puts the van in the product table "because we bought it". What's wrong?

*Q7 · Ch 3 · pricing*

b) Why does the €25 compete with the €30 but not with the €120?
