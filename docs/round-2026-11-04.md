Q5 · Ch 3 · estimated cost · (a) Apply, (b) Analyze, (c) Evaluate
We are Mesas Aliso, a furniture workshop in Zaragoza. We make an oak dining table and a
walnut dining table, and either can take an extension leaf (an option the customer picks).
Our estimated costs per unit:
- the oak table: oak wood 120, carpentry 90
- the extension leaf, on either table: hinges and runners 40, fitting 25
- from 1 Jan 2027 the oak wood rises to 140
- our subsidiary Aliso Portugal makes oak tables in its own workshop in Porto, where
  carpentry costs 60 (the rest is the same)
a) Write the estimate rows: what each is for, its type, the cost, the dates and any scope.
b) Aliso Portugal makes an oak table with the leaf in February 2027. Which rows give its
cost, and what is the total? Why those rows?
c) A colleague says: "Too many rows. Put a cost column on the product and one on the
feature." What can't that answer?

Q6 · Ch 2 · contact mechanisms, move vs typo · (a) Apply, (b) Analyze, (c) Understand
We are Gestoría Prado, an accounting firm. Since March 2024 our client Hermanos Vidal SL
and its manager, Elena Vidal, have both used Calle Mayor 12, Huesca: the company for its
invoices, Elena for her personal mail. On 1 Dec 2026 Elena moves, and her personal mail
goes to Avenida Pirineos 7, Huesca; the company stays at Calle Mayor 12. On 4 Dec we
notice Elena's new address was keyed as "Avenida Pireneos 7".
a) Write the rows as they stand before 1 Dec, then what 1 Dec adds, ends and leaves
untouched.
b) A colleague says: "On 1 Dec, just change Calle Mayor 12 to Avenida Pirineos 7." What
does that break?
c) How do you fix the 4 Dec mistake, and why not the way you handled 1 Dec?

Q7 · Ch 4 · order adjustments · (a) Apply, (b) Evaluate
We are Juguetería Nogal, a toy shop that sells online. Order 410 has two lines: line 1,
two wooden train sets at €45 each; line 2, one dolls' house at €120. On it:
- 10% off the train sets (a toy-fair promotion)
- €12 delivery for the whole order
- €6 extra on the delivery, because the address is on Mallorca
- IVA at 21% on the whole order
a) Write the adjustment rows: each one's type, whether it sits on the order or a line, the
amount or percentage, and its sign.
b) A colleague says: "Simpler: put discount, delivery, surcharge and tax columns on the
order." What does that break?
