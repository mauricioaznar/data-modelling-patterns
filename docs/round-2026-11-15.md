*Q1 · Ch 4 · requirements*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze
We are Hoteles Brisa. Our Cádiz hotel needs 40 new mattresses by 1 Apr 2027, budget €12,000. Marta Ruiz, the head of housekeeping, asked for them on 10 Jan 2027; Luis Gómez, our operations manager, answers for them.
a) Write the requirement row and its role rows.
b) A colleague's version has one column, requirement type, with four values (internal product, internal work, customer product, customer work). Write the filter for "every product need" on ours and on theirs. What does the difference cost?

*Q2 · Ch 4 · requirements*
(a) Apply · (b) Understand

a) Write the rows for the need and what they want.
b) Why do the incline and the monitor hang off the need, not off a product?

*Q3 · Ch 4 · orders, items and order parties*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze
We are Editorial Pino. Since we opened (1 Jan 2020), a sales order's salesperson must be our EMPLOYEE. From 1 Jul 2027 a CONTRACTOR may also be the salesperson. From 1 Jan 2028 employees no longer may.
a) Write the rule rows for the salesperson hat, with dates.
b) Sales order 770, dated 15 Feb 2028, has Ana, an employee, as its salesperson. What catches it, and which rule rows does it read?

*Q4 · Ch 4 · requirements*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze
We are Colegio Mar. The sports hall refit has two parts: new flooring and new lighting. Pilar Soto, the head teacher, answers for the refit; Jordi Vila, the caretaker, answers for the lighting. The refit is created on 10 Jan 2027 and approved on 20 Jan.
a) Write the role and status rows.
b) The lighting is done on 1 Mar. Write that row. Is the refit done, and what tells you?

*Q5 · Ch 3 · pricing*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze
We are Cafeteras Lume. The E5 espresso machine's base price is €300; the milk frother adds €40; customers in the Canary Islands pay a €25 surcharge.
a) Write the price rows. What does Rosa, in Tenerife, pay for an E5 with the frother?
b) Rosa orders it. Where does the €365 she agreed to live, and why not in a price column on the product?

*Q6 · Ch 4 · orders, items and order parties*
(a) Apply · (b) Understand
We are Viveros Norte. Sales order 750: Hotel Sol places it and pays for it; Eva, our clerk, keys it in; Tomás earns the sale. Line 1, 20 palm trees, goes to the hotel; line 2, 5 olive trees, goes to the hotel's owner, Carlos Díaz, at home.
a) Write the roles on the order and on each line.
b) Why does the ship-to sit on the line and not on the order?

*Q7 · Ch 4 · order adjustments and terms*
(a) Apply · (b) Understand
We are Imprenta Duna. Purchase order 760 to Papeles Río: line 1, 50 reams, delivered within 3 days; if line 1 is late, Río pays a 5% penalty. On the whole order, we pay within 30 days, plus a €15 delivery charge.
a) Write the rows for the 3 days, the 5%, the 30 days and the €15.
b) Why is the 5% not an adjustment?
