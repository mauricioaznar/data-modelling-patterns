Q5 · Ch 2 · specific vs generic relationships · (a) Analyze, (b) Apply, (c) Evaluate
Coworking Faro tracks four kinds of relationship with people: members, mentors, event
speakers and cleaning contractors, and new kinds keep coming. A membership has two
attributes that change over time: the plan (hot desk, fixed desk or private office) and
the monthly fee. Lucía has been a member since March 2023; she moved from hot desk to fixed
desk in 2024, and her fee rose in January 2025.
a) Generic party relationships, or a specific table per kind? Give the deciding factor.
b) Where do Lucía's plan and fee go so their history is kept? Name what each row hangs on.
c) A colleague says: "The relationship already has from and thru dates, so end Lucía's
membership and start a new one every time her plan or fee changes." What does that break?

Q6 · Ch 3 · reorder guidelines · (a) Apply, (b) Analyze
We are Papelería Mar, a chain of three stationery shops. For our black printer cartridge:
- chain-wide, reorder when stock falls to 20, ordering 50
- the Valencia shop sells far more: reorder at 40, ordering 100
- from 1 December the chain-wide level rises to 30 (the quantity stays 50)
a) Write the guideline rows: what each is for and scoped to, the level, the quantity and
the dates.
b) On 10 December the Valencia shop has 35 cartridges, and the Sagunto shop has 25. Which
row applies to each, and does either reorder? Why that row?

Q7 · Ch 2 · communication events and case · (a) Apply, (b) Understand
We are Taller Ruedas, a bike repair shop. On 3 Nov our customer Marta phones: her e-bike
battery keeps dying, and Andrés, our technician, takes the call and opens a case for it.
On 5 Nov she e-mails photos of the battery; from that e-mail Andrés schedules a battery
replacement job. On 12 Nov the battery is replaced and Marta confirms by phone that all is
well.
a) Write the rows: the case, each communication and how it joins the case, the job and
what it hangs on, and who plays which part.
b) How does the case end? What changes, and what doesn't?
