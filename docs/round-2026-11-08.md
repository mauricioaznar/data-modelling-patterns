*Q3 · Ch 3 · suppliers and inventory*
(a) Apply · (b) Evaluate

b) A colleague says: "Put the product on the lot, recalls get simpler." What can go wrong?

*Q4 · Ch 3 · pricing and costing*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze

b) A Haya chair with a cushion, made in Burgos on 10 Apr 2027: which rows count, and what's the total?

*Q6 · Ch 4 · order adjustments*

b) What's the order's total? What is the 10% taken from, and why?
