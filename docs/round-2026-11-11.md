*Q1 · Ch 2 · parties, roles and relationships*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze
We are Bodegas Cerro, a winery. Since 1 Mar 2026 Vinos Ruta sells our wine to restaurants and keeps 8% of each sale; it never buys a bottle. From 1 Feb 2027 it buys our wine by the pallet and resells it.
a) Write Vinos Ruta's role rows and relationship rows, before and after the switch: each relationship's type, from role → to role, and dates.
b) A colleague says: "Just change the role on the existing rows." What does that lose?

*Q2 · Ch 3 · suppliers and inventory*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze
We are Miel Sierra. Lot H-03 of our rosemary honey was made on 2 Oct 2026: 400 jars, best before 2 Oct 2028. 250 jars are on pallet P4 in our Teruel warehouse, 150 on the shelf of our Zaragoza shop.
a) Write the rows that record this stock.
b) 100 jars move from pallet P4 to the Zaragoza shelf. Which rows change, and which don't?

*Q3 · Ch 4 · orders, items and order parties*
(a) Analyze · (b) Evaluate
We are Taller Roble, a furniture workshop. Our rule: a sales order has one placing customer. Order 670 was keyed with two: Hotel Pinar and its owner, Grupo Pinar.
a) Where does the rule live in our model, and what happens to order 670's rows?
b) A colleague proposes a max-per-order column on the rule table, with the insert blocked when it's exceeded. Give one reason against the column, and one gain and one cost of blocking.

*Q4 · Ch 3 · product features*
(a) Apply · (b) Understand
We are Bicis Ría. Our Ría 7 e-bike always has its battery fitted. A rear rack comes on it, but you can ask us to take it off. Mudguards we fit only if you ask. The frame comes in M or L, and you must pick one.
a) Write how the battery, the rack, the mudguards and the frame sizes apply to the bike.
b) An order for a Ría 7 in M says nothing else. Does the bike leave with a rack, and with mudguards? Which row tells us?

*Q5 · Ch 4 · orders, items and order parties*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze
We are Papelería Arce. Sales order 680 from Colegio Pinar was placed by phone from 911 222 333 and is billed to the school's office, Calle Real 1. Line 1: 20 boxes of chalk to the main building, also Calle Real 1. Line 2: 5 whiteboards to the sports hall, Avenida Lago 9; ask for Marta on 655 444 333 when you get there.
a) Write where each address and phone goes.
b) Two days later the school says line 2 goes to Calle Real 1 as well. What changes, and what doesn't?

*Q6 · Ch 3 · product features*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze
We are Ópticas Brisa. On our Brisa frame, the blue-light coating needs thin lenses, and tinted lenses can't take the blue-light coating. On every frame we sell, polarized needs tinted lenses.
a) Write the interaction rows: type, the features, direction, and the product.
b) Why do the first two rules name the frame and the third doesn't? What goes wrong if the first one names no product?

*Q7 · Ch 3 · pricing*
(a) Apply · (b) Understand
We are Kayaks Delta. Our K2 kayak has these base prices: €800; €760 in Galicia; €740 for CLUB customers in Galicia. Rosa, a club member in Vigo (Galicia), and Ana, a club member in Madrid, each order a K2 today.
a) Which base price does each pay?
b) Why isn't Rosa's price 800 − 40 − 20, or any other sum of the three?
