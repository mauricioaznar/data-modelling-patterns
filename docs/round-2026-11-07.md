*Q1 · Ch 3 · pricing and costing*
(a) Apply · (b) Analyze · (c) Evaluate
We are Bicicletas Júcar, a workshop in Valencia that builds city bikes and folding bikes. Either bike can take an electric-assist kit, an option the customer picks. Since 1 Jan 2026 our estimated costs per unit are:
- the city bike: frame 150 (we buy the frames from Cuadros Ebro), assembly 70
- the e-assist kit, on either bike: motor and battery 300, fitting 40
- from 1 Mar 2027 the frame rises to 165
- our second workshop, Júcar Norte in Castellón, assembles city bikes for 55 (the rest is the same)
a) Write the estimate rows: what each is for, its type, the cost, the dates and any scope.
b) On 10 Apr 2027 Júcar Norte builds a city bike with the e-assist kit. Which rows give its cost, and what is the total? Why those rows?
c) A colleague says: "We buy the frames from Cuadros Ebro, so put the 150 on their supplier row." What's wrong with that?

*Q2 · Ch 4 · orders*
