## The five lines to remember
1. Every line: **who** (role) **and where** (contact mechanism with a purpose).
2. **The buyer places, the seller takes.** Every other hat answers its own question.
3. Rule row = **shape**, check query = **count**; a rule row does nothing without its check.
4. The outside party points at us; the person points at the organization; employment starts from us.
5. Off = × 0.9; feature prices add; the most specific base wins.
